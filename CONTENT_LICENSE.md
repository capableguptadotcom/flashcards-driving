The Europe curriculum uses original educational wording based on public factual material from official European Union and national-authority sources. Each Europe card links to the authority used for that claim; the source audit is at `research/europe-official-source-audit.md`. The supplied 2016 travel handbook informed the topic map only and is not treated as current legal authority.

The India curriculum uses original educational wording based on the Motor Vehicles Act, national driving regulations and official Indian government services. Each card cites its source; the evidence and limitations are recorded in `research/india-official-source-audit.md`. Government source documents retain their own applicable terms. Official DMV image provenance is recorded in `assets/dmv/README.md`.

The project is independent and is not affiliated with or endorsed by the California Department of Motor Vehicles, the European Union, MoRTH, Parivahan, or any national road authority. Rules can change; verify them against the current official source for the place where you will drive before relying on this study aid.
