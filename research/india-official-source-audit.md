# India national-core source audit

Reviewed: **17 October 2026**. Scope: 60 original English teaching cards across six decks for ordinary private-car and motorcycle learners. This is a national core, not an exhaustive state learner-test question bank, transport-driver qualification guide, or a certified consolidation of legislation.

- **H — Current motorcycle framework cross-check:** [MoRTH's answer to Parliament on two-wheeler safety](https://sansad.in/getFile/annex/268/AU2029_plbDgn.pdf?source=pqars) confirms sections 128/129 and the age-four child distinction. ## Decisions and limitations

1. **Statute beats a loose FAQ summary.** Parivahan's FAQ shorthand says age16 for MCWOG. The legal age exception is the <=50cc rule in section4; the scenario explicitly rejects a110cc automatic scooter. It does not teach that any gearless scooter qualifies.
2. **The 2026 amendment was checked.** Schedule51 does not alter the age16/18 rules, class entitlement, section13 all-India validity, six-month learner validity in section14(1), section128/129 adult motorcycle rules, section134 reporting/help duties, section146 insurance requirement, or section185 threshold used here. It does introduce a30-day continuation after full driving-licence expiry through section14(2), among other changes. This must not be extended to an expired learner's licence under section14(1). Cards avoid full-licence renewal advice and all penalty amounts. They do not repeat obsolete criminal-penalty wording from the older Act PDF.
3. **2017 alone is insufficient for phones/documents.** The2020 amendment is used, rather than the old blanket handheld wording or paper-only transport documents.
4. **Safety explanations are editorial applications.** For example, a horn cannot reveal oncoming traffic, an indicator does not create priority, and unsafe triangle placement should not expose a person to traffic. These explain how to apply the rules, not add new national legal thresholds.
5. **National coverage is deliberately bounded.** No universal exam count, passing mark, application fee, state fine, PUC renewal interval, commercial-licence entitlement, electric-scooter exemption or claim that all tests can be completed from home is included. No invented fixed passing distance, general expressway speed or nationwide child-seat installation requirement is asserted.
6. **Not yet covered in these60 cards:** full road-sign recognition bank, child motorcycle harness details, detailed child-restraint scenarios, transport/hazardous-goods qualifications, state-specific practical-test manoeuvres, licence renewals and accident compensation claims. These require dedicated additional sourcing; they must not be inferred from this core.
7. **Date meaning.** `verifiedOn: 2026-10-17` records this editorial review against the cited accessible evidence. It does not certify that every government PDF is an up-to-date consolidation or that no newer state notification exists. Broken or empty source responses must be reported as unavailable during future reviews, never silently counted as unchanged.
8. **A recently dated government page can still contain old answers.** The Delhi FAQ footer says updated16October2026, but its learner-renewal and full-licence-validity answers conflict with more recent central guidance. Only its concrete car-supervision/L-sign guidance is used. Do not import its fee, renewal, same-day issue or validity statements into this deck. The claim that a supervisor must be positioned to control or stop the car was omitted because full rule3 retrieval was unavailable; the retained supervision and L-sign details were read directly on the official page.

## Monthly maintenance checks

- Review MoRTH final legislation and Gazette notifications, India Code's current Act, and the state's Sarathi instructions before applying changes.
- Re-check2026 Schedule51 commencement against the official notification PDF when access is restored. Keep enacted text, draft proposals, commencement and state implementation distinct.
- Diff relevant source text and inspect the actual section/regulation rather than treating website timestamps or raw PDF hashes as legal changes.
- Change or retire affected cards with explicit source/locator and effective date. Increment the card's `revision` for meaning changes, preserve its stable ID for corrections, and reset stale mastery when the UI supports revision-aware progress.
- If an official source is unavailable, flag the affected claim for follow-up and retain the last verified wording unless there is concrete conflicting evidence. Do not promote a search snippet or news story into a legal update.
