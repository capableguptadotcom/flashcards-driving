## India: new proposal, not a rule to teach as current

The [official Gazette PDF, CG-DL-E-21102026-277305](https://egazette.gov.in/WriteReadData/2026/277305.pdf) was successfully downloaded on 1 November 2026 (51 pages, 2,974,848 bytes). The web reader timed out, but direct HTTPS retrieval succeeded; English printed pages 30-34 were read with PDF text extraction.

- Printed p.30 identifies **G.S.R. 902(E), dated 16 October 2026**, as draft amendments to the Central Motor Vehicles Rules. The Gazette issue date is **21 October 2026**. It invites objections/suggestions for thirty days from availability to the public. The draft's proposed commencement is on **final publication**, not automatically on the draft's issue date.
- Printed p.31 proposes changing rule 11's test threshold from 60 to 75 percent and inserting a safe-driving tutorial duration of at least thirty minutes. Pages 30-34 also propose application evidence, licence-format and administrative changes.
- Decision: keep `india-licence-07` and `india-licence-08` as State/RTO checks. Do **not** add 75 percent as an already-effective national answer. No final-notification date was established in this review. Watch for the final Gazette text and state/Sarathi implementation at the next check.

The [MoRTH 2020 electronic-enforcement release](https://www.pib.gov.in/PressReleaseIframePage.aspx?PRID=1659408) was fetched and read. It continues to support `india-safe-04`, `india-safe-07` and `india-safe-08`: navigation-only use must not disturb concentration, and validated electronic documents need not be demanded physically. Its publication date is 26 September 2020 and stated effect date is 1 October 2020; fetching it today is not evidence of a new amendment.

## Europe: seasonal scope remains correct

[France's official Service-Public notice](https://www.service-public.gouv.fr/particuliers/actualites/A14389), published **20 October 2026**, was read in full. It describes the recurring winter-equipment period **1 November 2026 through 31 March 2027** in designated mountain communes. For the private light vehicles in its example, it explains the choice of suitable removable equipment for at least two driven wheels or four qualifying winter tyres; the scope depends on designated and signed zones. It is not an EU-wide winter-tyre obligation.

Decision: `borders-07`, `conditions-01`, `conditions-03` and `conditions-04` already require destination/date/condition checks rather than teaching a universal European threshold. No amendment to those generic answers is required by this seasonal reminder. A detailed France pack remains outside this monthly maintenance change.
